<h2>📌 Project Overview</h2>

<p>
The <strong>Sales & Profit Dashboard</strong> is an interactive Microsoft Excel dashboard
designed to analyze sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, product categories,
monthly trends, and geographical sales distribution.
</p>

<p>
The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights using
<strong>Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, KPIs, Data Visualization, and Excel Dashboard Design techniques.</strong>
</p>

<p>
The main purpose of this project is to provide a clear and interactive overview of
sales and profit performance so that business users can quickly identify trends,
high-performing categories, profitable customers, and regional performance.
</p>

---

<h2>🎯 Project Objectives</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Analyze overall sales performance.</li>
  <li>Measure total profit generated by the business.</li>
  <li>Compare yearly profit performance.</li>
  <li>Identify top-performing product categories.</li>
  <li>Analyze monthly sales trends.</li>
  <li>Identify customers generating the highest profit.</li>
  <li>Understand customer distribution by year.</li>
  <li>Analyze sales performance across different states.</li>
  <li>Create an interactive dashboard using Excel slicers.</li>
  <li>Present complex data in an easy-to-understand visual format.</li>
</ul>

<h2>🎛️ Interactive Filters</h2>

<p>
The dashboard contains interactive slicers that allow users to filter the entire dashboard
and analyze specific segments of the data.
</p>

<h3>Available Filters</h3>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Year:</strong> 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024</li>
  <li><strong>Category:</strong> Furniture, Office Supplies, Technology</li>
  <li><strong>Month:</strong> January to December</li>
</ul>

<p>
These filters make the dashboard dynamic and allow users to explore different
time periods, categories, and monthly performance without manually changing the data.
</p>

<h2>📊 Dashboard Components</h2>

The Total Sales KPI displays the overall sales revenue generated by the business.

<h2>🎨 Dashboard Design</h2>

<p>
The dashboard follows a clean and professional business intelligence style.
A blue-based color scheme is used to maintain visual consistency and improve readability.
</p>

<p>
The dashboard layout is divided into different analytical sections so that users can
quickly understand the most important business information.
</p>

<ul>
  <li>Top section → Main KPIs</li>
  <li>Left section → Interactive filters</li>
  <li>Center section → Sales and profit analysis</li>
  <li>Right section → Customer and geographical analysis</li>
  <li>Bottom section → Customer profitability and monthly sales trends</li>
</ul>

  <li>Open the workbook using Microsoft Excel.</li>
  <li>Navigate to the Dashboard sheet.</li>
